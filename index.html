About Us

Where Every Child's Potential is Unleashed

Founded in 2009, Larbec International Academy has grown from a modest neighborhood school to one of Kumasi's most respected educational institutions. Our journey has been marked by consistent academic achievement, innovative teaching methods, and an unwavering commitment to student success.

We believe education should be transformative, not just transactional. Our philosophy centers on developing curious, compassionate, and capable young individuals who are prepared to excel in an increasingly complex world.

Academics

Our Academic Excellence

We offer tailored educational pathways for every stage of your child's development, from Crèche through Junior High School with specialized programs that nurture intellectual and moral growth.

Crèche Program
Ages 6 months to 2 years with Montessori-inspired learning, sensory development, and early socialization in a warm, nurturing environment.
Kindergarten
Ages 3 to 5 years with play-based curriculum, early literacy and numeracy, and creative expression through music and arts.
Primary School
Ages 6 to 11 years with core subject mastery, STEM introduction, and character development programs.
Junior High School
Ages 12 to 15 years with BECE preparation, advanced STEM, and leadership training for academic success.
STEM Focus
Specialized Science, Technology, Engineering, and Mathematics programs from primary level with hands-on laboratories.
Holistic Development
We nurture intellectual, physical, emotional, and social growth through comprehensive programs and character education.

Why Choose Larbec International Academy

May 20, 2026

Discover what makes Larbec International Academy the preferred choice for discerning parents in Kumasi and beyond. Our commitment to excellence spans every aspect of our institution.

Academic Excellence: With a 98% BECE pass rate over the last 5 years and 85% of students scoring 6+ aggregates, we consistently rank among the top schools in the Ashanti Region. Our 1:15 teacher-to-student ratio ensures personalized attention and support.

STEM Focus: We offer specialized Science, Technology, Engineering, and Mathematics programs from primary level, equipped with modern laboratories and experienced instructors preparing students for the future.

Holistic Development: Beyond academics, we nurture intellectual, physical, emotional, and social growth through comprehensive programs that develop character, confidence, and leadership skills.

World-Class Facilities: Our academy boasts a modern library with 5,000+ volumes, fully equipped science laboratories, state-of-the-art ICT center, athletic facilities, creative arts studio, and a nutrition center.
