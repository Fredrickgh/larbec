Access Your Student Portal

Step 1: Go to Student Portal

Click the "Go to Student Portal" button below, or visit student-portal.html

Step 2: Sign In

Log in using one of these options:
Option A: Your Admission Code (shown above)
Option B: Parent email (the email used in this application)

Step 3: Track & Manage

View your application status, receive notifications from the admissions team, and download your acceptance/rejection letter once a decision is made

Timeline

  • 📋 Within 24 hours: Your application is logged into our system and assigned a case number
  • ⏱️ 5–7 working days: Admissions team reviews your complete application
  • 📧 1-2 weeks: Initial decision made (you'll see notification in your portal)
  • 📄 Upon acceptance: Download your official Admission Letter from your Student Portal

Entrance Assessment

Selected applicants may be invited to complete an entrance assessment as part of our admissions process. This helps us understand your learning style and academic readiness.

📍 All entrance assessment details, including dates and instructions, will be communicated directly through your Student Portal once you've logged in with your Admission Code. Check your portal regularly for updates!
